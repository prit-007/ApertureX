ApertureX is a mobile-native, non-destructive, free and open-source photo
editor — a touch-first alternative to Snapseed and Lightroom.

Two interfaces over one edit recipe:
* Panels (Lightroom-style) — categorized sliders, tone curves, an HSL mixer,
  geometry, masks, and a node-based local-adjustment editor.
* Gestures (Snapseed-style) — an edge-to-edge ghost UI: swipe for tools, scrub
  to adjust, drag the photo to target a colour (HSL), pinch to zoom.

A real color engine in Rust, fast enough to feel instant:
* Light, Color, Effects and Detail groups; split toning; curves; HSL mixer.
* Crop with aspect presets, rotate, flip, straighten, and keystone correction.
* Clarity, texture, sharpen, denoise, dehaze, vignette, and film grain.
* Local adjustments with linear, radial, luminance, colour-range, and AI
  subject masks.

Built for a photographer's workflow:
* A date-grouped gallery with albums, batch editing, and a searchable library.
* Non-destructive recipes stored per photo; import .cube LUTs, Lightroom .xmp
  presets, and shareable JSON looks.
* Full-resolution JPEG, PNG, and 16-bit TIFF export with EXIF preserved.
* On-device AI for face detection, subject masks, depth, and lens blur.

ApertureX is fully offline. It has no ads, accounts, telemetry, or network
calls; your photos and settings stay on your device. Licensed GPL-3.0-or-later.

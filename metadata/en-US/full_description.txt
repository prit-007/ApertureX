ApertureX is a mobile-native, non-destructive, free and open-source photo
editor — a touch-first alternative to Snapseed and Lightroom.

Two interfaces over one edit recipe:
* Panels (Lightroom-style) — categorized sliders, tone curves, an HSL mixer,
  geometry, and masks.
* Gestures (Snapseed-style) — an edge-to-edge "ghost" UI: swipe for tools,
  scrub to adjust, drag the photo to target a colour (HSL), pinch to zoom.

A real color engine (Rust), fast enough to feel instant:
* Light, Color, Effects and Detail groups; split toning; curves; HSL mixer.
* Crop with aspect presets, rotate, flip, straighten.
* Clarity, texture, sharpen, denoise, dehaze, vignette, grain.
* Local adjustments with linear/radial/luminance masks.

Built for a photographer's workflow:
* A date-grouped gallery with albums and batch editing.
* Non-destructive recipes stored per photo; import .cube LUTs, Lightroom .xmp
  presets, and shareable JSON looks.
* Full-resolution export with quality/size options, EXIF preserved, share.

ApertureX is fully offline. It makes no network calls and collects nothing;
your photos and settings stay on your device.
